Frequently Asked Questions

What is the check-in time?

Check-in time is 12:00 PM. Early check-in is subject to room availability.

What is the check-out time?

Check-out time is 11:00 AM. Guests can contact reception for late check-out requests.

Does the hotel provide free Wi-Fi?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available for guests throughout the hotel.

Is breakfast included with the room?

Breakfast depends on the selected room package. Please check your booking details.

Does the hotel provide airport pickup?

Yes, airport pickup can be arranged on request. Additional charges may apply.

Is parking available at the hotel?

Yes. Secure parking is available for hotel guests.

Does the hotel have a swimming pool?

Yes. Grand Core2Dine Hotel has a swimming pool for guests.

Is room service available 24x7?

Yes, room service is available 24 hours a day for your convenience.

How can I book a room?

You can use our online booking form to select your room and provide your stay details.

How can I contact the hotel?

You can contact us by phone at +91 7021934579 or email us at info@grandcore2dine.example.
